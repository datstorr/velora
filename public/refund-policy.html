Refund & Cancellation Policy

Change-of-Mind Returns

For eligible returns relating to size or color preference, VELORA generally provides an exchange or Store Credit rather than a refund to the original payment method.

Damaged or Defective Products

If your product arrives damaged or has a qualifying quality issue, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photographic or video evidence.

Order Cancellation

To cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible. Cancellation requests made before shipment will be reviewed where possible; once an order has shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.

Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved or legally required, it will be processed using the applicable payment method. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider or financial institution.

Contact us at datstorr@gmail.com for any refund or cancellation request.
