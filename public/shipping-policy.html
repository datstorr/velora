Shipping Policy

Delivery Timeline: Orders are typically delivered within 8-10 business days from the date of shipping.

Express/Emergency Delivery: Need it sooner? We offer expedited delivery for an additional cost, subject to availability and delivery location. Contact us before placing your order to check if faster delivery is possible for your area.

Order Cancellations: Once an order is placed, it cannot be cancelled for a refund. If you wish to cancel, compensation will be provided only in the form of a gift card for the order amount, in line with our Refund Policy.

Tracking: You'll receive a tracking link via email once your order has shipped.
