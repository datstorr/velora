Shipping Policy

We want your VELORA order to reach you safely and as smoothly as possible.

Processing & Delivery

Orders are generally delivered within 7 to 8 business days. Delivery estimates may vary depending on destination, order circumstances, courier operations, holidays, weather, and other circumstances outside our reasonable control.

Tracking

Once your order has shipped, tracking information will be provided when available.

Shipping Delays

While we work to meet our estimated delivery timeframe, unexpected delays can occur. If we determine that an order cannot be shipped within the timeframe promised, we will contact you and provide the options required by applicable law, which may include accepting the delay or cancelling for a refund.

Incorrect Shipping Information

Please carefully check your shipping information before completing your purchase. If an order cannot be delivered because an incorrect or incomplete address was provided, contact us as soon as possible.

Lost or Damaged Shipments

If your package appears to have been lost or arrives damaged, contact us with your order number and relevant photographs. We will review and assist with next steps.

International Orders

For international orders, the customer may be responsible for applicable customs duties, taxes, or other charges imposed by the destination country unless otherwise stated at checkout.
