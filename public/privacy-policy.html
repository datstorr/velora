Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Velora ("we," "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit or shop on our website.

How We Use Your Information

To process and deliver orders, communicate updates, and (only if opted in) send promotional messages.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your data. We only share it with trusted partners necessary to run our business — shipping/courier partners and our payment processor.

Your Rights

You can request to access, correct, or delete your personal data by contacting us.
