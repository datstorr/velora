Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be happy with your VELORA purchase. If something isn't right, please contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll do our best to help.

Size or Color

If you'd prefer a different size or color, contact us to request an exchange, subject to availability. If you prefer to return the item instead, an eligible return may be issued as VELORA Store Credit rather than a refund to the original payment method, usable toward a future purchase.

Return Requirements

To be eligible, the item must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, free from stains or odors, and in its original condition and packaging where applicable.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Products

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or materially different from what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number, a description of the issue, and clear photographs (and a video if helpful).

Important

Please do not send an item back before contacting us first. If approved, we'll provide instructions on what to do next.
