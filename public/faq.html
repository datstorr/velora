Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Orders are generally delivered within 7-8 business days. Courier, weather, or holiday delays may occasionally affect this.

Will I receive tracking?

Yes, once your order ships, tracking details will be provided where available.

Can I change my size or color?

Contact us as soon as possible. Exchanges are subject to availability.

Can I return my order?

Eligible returns for size or color preference are generally issued as VELORA Store Credit rather than a refund.

What if my product arrives damaged or defective?

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos or video of the issue.

Can I cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible — cancellation may be possible before shipment, but can't be guaranteed after.
